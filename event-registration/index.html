Event Registration

A powerful combination of registration products and services, backed by deployment know-how, to implement complete market-ready systems and generate ROI.

Onsite and online

One system, not two products bolted together

The RegBox™ all-in-one onsite registration system integrates visitor and delegate registration, tracking, reporting and barcoded ID badge printing in a range of card formats — designed to stay straightforward for desk staff under pressure, and to produce personalised ID badges at a fraction of the usual investment.
