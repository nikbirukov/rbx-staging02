Live, not retrospective

Data while your event is still running

With Event Deck, you have live access to data being generated at your event: from pre-event marketing results to real-time attendance figures and exhibitor performance. With instant access to usable data, you can respond quickly to problems, feed back to sponsors, and make improvements while there is still time to act on them.
