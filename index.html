Most registration problems are two systems disagreeing

An online tool and a separate door system mean two lists

An import on the morning of the show, and a reconciliation afterwards that never quite balances. RegBox is one system. The record an attendee creates is the record the desk admits and the record an exhibitor's scanner reads — so the leads a stand takes home are the same verified data the organiser owns.

Typical stack

Two lists, one reconciliation

Registration exports a spreadsheet. The door system imports it. Numbers drift the moment either list changes, and someone spends the Monday after the show working out whose count was right.
